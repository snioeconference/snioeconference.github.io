Hands-on Epigenetics Workshop

SNIoE, 2026

We are pleased to announce a fully sponsored five-day hands-on workshop on Cut&Tag and ATAC-Seq workflows, organized by the Center of Excellence in Epigenetics (COEE), Department of Life Sciences, Shiv Nadar Institution of Eminence (SNIoE), and sponsored by Sun Pharma Science Foundation.

The workshop will be held from July 13–17 and will provide intensive practical training covering the complete experimental workflow from sample preparation to sequencing-ready library generation.

Participants will receive hands-on training in:

  • Cell suspension preparation
  • Sample quality control (QC)
  • Cut&Tag library preparation
  • ATAC-Seq library preparation
  • Preparation of sequencing-ready libraries
  • Library quality assessment and QC

The workshop is designed as an end-to-end experimental training program, emphasizing practical execution, troubleshooting, and quality-control checkpoints at every stage of the workflow.

All experimental reagents, accommodation, and meals will be fully covered for selected participants.

Due to the intensive hands-on nature of the training, participation will be limited to 25 participants. Kindly [register](https://snioeconference.github.io/registration/) your interest.

We look forward to welcoming you to the Workshop!
