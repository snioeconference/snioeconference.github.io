Workshop Program

Day 1 Morning Session: Introduction, Sample Handling and Contamination Control
Introduction to experimental workflows; contamination prevention strategies; sample tracking, labeling, and pipetting best practices.
Afternoon Session: Cell Preparation Workflows
Preparation of cultured cells and tissue-derived samples; cell counting and viability assessment; optimization of cell handling prior to downstream chromatin assays.
Evening Session: Quality Control using Microscopy
QC assessment using brightfield and fluorescent microscopy; evaluation of cell integrity and sample quality; troubleshooting common preparation artifacts.
Day 2 CUT&Tag – Part I (Primary Antibody Binding)
Cell preparation and immobilization; permeabilization; antibody incubation and optimization strategies; discussion of chromatin accessibility and background reduction.
Day 3 CUT&Tag – Part II (Library Preparation)
Tagmentation and cleanup steps; PCR amplification and indexing strategy; library QC and troubleshooting of low-yield or over-amplified libraries.
Day 4 ATAC-seq – Start to End
Nuclei isolation and transposition reaction setup; library amplification and fragment enrichment; interpretation of nucleosomal periodicity and QC metrics.
Day 5 Library QC using TapeStation
Fragment size distribution analysis; identification of successful vs failed libraries; pooling logic, sequencing readiness, and troubleshooting discussion.
