Workshop Program

Day 1 Morning Session: Introduction, Sample Handling and Contamination Control
Introduction to experimental workflows; contamination prevention strategies; sample tracking, labeling, and pipetting best practices.
Afternoon Session: Cell Preparation Workflows
Preparation of cultured cells and tissue-derived samples; cell counting and viability assessment; optimization of cell handling prior to downstream chromatin assays.
Evening Session: Quality Control using Microscopy
QC assessment using brightfield and fluorescent microscopy; evaluation of cell integrity and sample quality; troubleshooting common preparation artifacts.
Day 2 Morning Session: Introduction to CUT&Tag + Guest Lecture
Overview of CUT&Tag principles and workflow; discussion of chromatin profiling strategies; external expert lecture on applications, experimental design, and common technical pitfalls.
Afternoon Session: CUT&Tag – Cell Preparation
Cell preparation and immobilization; permeabilization workflows; optimization of sample handling and permeability prior to antibody incubation.
Evening Session: Washes and Primary Antibody Binding
Wash steps to minimize background signal; primary antibody incubation; troubleshooting antibody specificity and sample quality issues.
Day 3 Morning Session: Secondary Antibody Binding and pA-Tn5 Incubation
Secondary antibody incubation; pA-Tn5 binding and handling; optimization of reaction conditions and reduction of non-specific background.
Afternoon Session: Tagmentation and Library Amplification
Tagmentation activation and cleanup steps; PCR amplification and indexing strategy; optimization of amplification cycles and library yield.
Evening Session: Library QC and Troubleshooting
Assessment of library concentration and fragment profiles; identification of low-yield or over-amplified libraries; troubleshooting and preparation for downstream sequencing.
Day 4 Morning Session: Introduction to ATAC-seq + Guest Lecture
Overview of ATAC-seq principles and chromatin accessibility profiling; external expert lecture on experimental design, applications of ATAC-seq, and common technical challenges in open chromatin analysis.
Afternoon Session: Nuclei Isolation and Transposition Reaction
Nuclei isolation workflows and quality assessment; transposition reaction setup; optimization of nuclei handling and reduction of sample debris.
Evening Session: Library Amplification and QC Metrics
Library amplification and fragment enrichment; interpretation of nucleosomal periodicity patterns; troubleshooting library quality and yield issues.
Day 5 Morning Session: Library QC using TapeStation
Fragment size distribution analysis; identification of successful vs failed libraries; pooling logic, sequencing readiness, and troubleshooting discussion.
Afternoon Session: Workshop Review, Feedback and Closing Remarks
Review of workflows and troubleshooting strategies covered during the workshop; participant feedback session; discussion on implementation in home laboratories; certificate distribution and closing remarks.
